Behind the Kitchen

Crafted with Heart, Served with Warmth

MiniKitchen was born from an unshakeable belief that exceptional food doesn’t need rigid formalities. We celebrate the intersection of soulful neighborhood comfort with rigorous Michelin-trained culinary technique.

Every single morning, our kitchen team kneads 48-hour sourdough, simmers rich tonkotsu broth for 18 hours, and sources herbs from nearby rooftop gardens.
